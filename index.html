    How it works

    A small test interface for ranking billboard locations in Greater Beirut.

    1. Pick a layer: Traffic, Speed, People or Places.
    2. Click any road to place a test board there, facing that direction.
    3. Check the view: blue dots mark road spots with a clear view of the board, red dots mark spots where a building blocks it. Each spot within 250 m is tested with a straight sight line to the board against OpenStreetMap building footprints.
    4. Read the panel: the board is ranked against other roads for traffic, time in view and people nearby.
    5. Explore in 3D or switch to Satellite to check the surroundings.
